## Evaluator behaviour that differs from the rule's own numbers

A doxorubicin patient at 1.5 mg/dL (about 26 micromol/L, a 50% dose per the monograph) is shown as ready. *Test:* AC regimen, bilirubin 1.5 mg/dL, measured 3 days ago → expect fail or dose-modification flag, not pass.
- **R-HN-03** `backend/sql/procedures/evaluate_gates.sql:373-402` - The rule's JSON says 60 for cisplatin, but the evaluator ignores the regimen and passes anyone at 30 or above ('meets carboplatin/capecitabine 30 minimum only'). - **R-GO-03** `backend/sql/procedures/evaluate_gates.sql:373-402` - The rule's JSON (60) is stricter than this protocol (50), but the evaluator actually passes anyone at 30 or above. A cervix patient at 40 mL/min is shown as ready. *Test:* cisplatin regimen, creatinine clearance 40 mL/min → expect fail, not pass.
- **R-LU-03** `backend/sql/procedures/evaluate_gates.sql:373-402` - The rule's JSON (45) matches the protocol, but the evaluator passes anyone at 30 or above. ## Unsafe - Saarthi can pass a patient the source says to hold or reduce (5)

**R-AC-03** · AC · DOXO_MONO p.9 · rules: `CLIN-BILI-001`  
> >3 x ULN or 20-50 50% … - >85 do not administer  
Doxorubicin is cleared by the liver. If bilirubin (a liver blood test) is 20-50 micromol/L (about 1.2-2.9 mg/dL), halve the dose; above 85, do not give it. Full dose only below 20 micromol/L. *No freshness limit either: a bilirubin from months ago counts.*

**R-TH-03** · TH · BRAJTTW p.5 · rules: `CLIN-BILI-001`  
> Less than 10 x ULN and Less than or equal to 1.25 x ULN 80  
Full-dose paclitaxel needs bilirubin no higher than 1.25 times the lab's upper normal limit. Between 1.26 and 2 times, the dose drops from 80 to 60. *The rule allows up to 1.5 x ULN for paclitaxel; the protocol reduces the dose above 1.25 x ULN.*

**R-HN-03** · CIS-RT-HN · HNLAPRT p.3 · rules: `CLIN-CRCL-001`  
> Less than 45 Hold CISplatin or delay with additional fluids  
Cisplatin can damage the kidneys. Full dose at creatinine clearance 60 or more, 80% dose at 45-59, and hold below 45.

**R-GO-03** · CIS-RT-CX · GOCXCRT p.2 · rules: `CLIN-CRCL-001`  
> Less than 50 mL/minute Delay chemotherapy, recheck in 1 week  
Delay cisplatin if creatinine clearance is below 50 mL/min.

**R-LU-03** · PEM-CARBO · LUAVPP p.2 · rules: `CLIN-CRCL-001`  
> Less than 45 Hold
