# Complete Patient Journey — HER2+ Breast Cancer, India

**The story of one patient from first symptom to one year post-treatment. Every visit, every test, every document, every delay, every cost, every person involved.**

> **Why this exists**: The synthetic data generator must produce records that trace a real journey, not abstract data points. Every row in every table must map to a moment in this timeline. If a record can't be placed on this map, it shouldn't exist in the database.

---

## The patient

**Meera** (synthetic, composited from published Indian oncology data):
- Age 47, lives in Jhansi (Uttar Pradesh, tier-2 city)
- School teacher, husband works in a government office
- Two children (19 and 15)
- Has PM-JAY coverage (BPL category). No private insurance.
- Languages: Hindi (primary), basic English literacy
- The family's total savings: approximately ₹2 lakh

She will travel 650 km to a hub hospital in Delhi for treatment. Her husband is the caretaker — he carries the folder, navigates the hospitals, talks to insurance, and tracks appointments. When he can't come, her sister (who lives in Delhi) substitutes.

## Phase 1: First symptom to diagnosis (Weeks 0–8)

### Week 0 — Discovery
**What happens**: Meera notices a lump in her left breast while bathing. Waits 2 weeks before telling her husband. He insists on seeing a doctor.

**The delay**: Published data (PMC12374521) shows median patient delay (symptom to first consultation) in Indian breast cancer is **2-3 months**. Meera's 2-week delay is better than average. Reasons for delay: denial, stigma, cost fear, no awareness of urgency, distance to a doctor who can evaluate.

### Week 2 — First consultation (spoke: District Hospital, Jhansi)
**Who**: General surgeon at the district hospital OPD
**Visit type**: Walk-in OPD, ₹10-50 registration

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | OPD registration slip | Paper (carbon copy) | Registration clerk | Name, age, MRN (spoke), address, phone | Immediately |
| D2 | Consultation note | Handwritten on OPD card | General surgeon | Chief complaint, exam findings ("2×3 cm firm mass, left breast, upper outer quadrant"), advice: "mammography + USG" | Immediately (in patient's hand) |

**What goes into the folder**: D1, D2 (originals — there is no copy in the hospital system at most district hospitals)

**Cost**: ₹50 registration + ₹500-1000 consultation = ~₹1,000

### Week 3 — Imaging (spoke: private diagnostic centre, Jhansi)
**Why private**: District hospital doesn't have a mammography machine, or the wait is 3-4 weeks.

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D3 | Mammogram report | Printed on letterhead, with film/CD | Radiologist | BIRADS 5 ("highly suggestive of malignancy"), 2.5 cm mass, location, recommendation: "biopsy advised" | 1-2 days |
| D4 | Breast ultrasound report | Printed on letterhead, with images | Radiologist | Solid hypoechoic mass 2.5×2.1 cm, irregular margins, axillary lymph node 1.5 cm | Same day or next day |

**What goes into the folder**: D3, D4 (originals). Meera's husband also takes phone photos (D3-photo, D4-photo) and WhatsApps them to a relative who is a nurse in Delhi for advice.

**Cost**: ₹2,000 mammogram + ₹800 USG = ~₹2,800

### Week 4 — Biopsy (spoke: District Hospital, Jhansi)
**Who**: General surgeon performs core needle biopsy under USG guidance
**Specimen sent to**: External pathology lab (Jhansi doesn't have an oncopathologist)

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D5 | Biopsy consent form | Pre-printed paper, signed | Surgeon + patient | Procedure name, risks, patient signature, witness | Day of procedure |
| D6 | Biopsy request form | Paper (sent with specimen) | Surgeon | Clinical details, specimen type ("core needle biopsy, left breast"), provisional diagnosis | Sent with specimen |
| D7 | Specimen acknowledgment | Paper slip or SMS | Lab | Specimen ID / accession number, receipt date | 1-2 days |

**The specimen travels**: physically, by courier or by the patient's family member, to a pathology lab. May be in Jhansi, or may be sent to Lucknow or Delhi for IHC/FISH.

**Cost**: ₹3,000-5,000 biopsy procedure + ₹2,000-4,000 pathology (basic HPE) = ~₹6,000

### Week 5-6 — Pathology results

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D8 | Histopathology report (preliminary) | Printed report, 2-3 pages | Pathologist | "Invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 2, Nottingham score 6/9" + margins + LVI + Ki-67. **NO IHC yet** — the report says "IHC panel to follow" or "Addendum for receptor status" | 5-7 working days after specimen receipt |
| D9 | IHC report (addendum or separate) | Printed report, 1-2 pages | Pathologist | ER: positive (80%), PR: positive (40%), HER2: **2+ (equivocal)**, Ki-67: 30%. **"FISH recommended for HER2 confirmation"** | 3-5 working days after D8 |

**THE CRITICAL GAP**: D9 says HER2 is 2+ (equivocal) and FISH is needed. But the lab in Jhansi doesn't do FISH. The specimen must be sent to a referral lab in Delhi or Mumbai. The family doesn't know this. Nobody tells them proactively. The surgeon says "go to a cancer hospital in Delhi for treatment, take all your reports."

**What's in the folder now**: D1-D4 (originals), D5 (consent copy), D8, D9. No FISH result. The pathology report says "addendum to follow" for HER2 — but no one is tracking that addendum.

**Cost**: ₹3,000-5,000 for IHC panel

**Time elapsed**: 6 weeks from first symptom. No treatment has started.

### Week 6-7 — Referral decision
The surgeon in Jhansi refers Meera to a hub cancer hospital in Delhi. Options: Rajiv Gandhi Cancer Institute, AIIMS Delhi, Safdarjung Hospital. The family chooses based on: (a) PM-JAY empanelment, (b) a relative in Delhi who can provide accommodation.

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D10 | Referral letter | Handwritten on letterhead, or typed | General surgeon | "Referred for further management of Ca breast. Reports enclosed." May or may not list which reports are enclosed. | Same day |

**What the family must arrange**: Train tickets (₹2,000-3,000 return × 2 people), accommodation in Delhi (relative's house or dharamshala near hospital), leave from work/school.

## Phase 2: Hub hospital — diagnosis confirmation (Weeks 7–10)

### Week 7 — First visit to hub hospital (Delhi)
**Who**: Oncology OPD registration → Surgical oncologist or medical oncologist

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D11 | Registration form (hub) | Digital (hospital EMR) | Registration desk | **New MRN (different from spoke MRN)**. Name, age, address, phone, ABHA (if available — likely not), PM-JAY card number | Immediately |
| D12 | PM-JAY eligibility verification | Digital (TMS portal printout) | PM-JAY desk | Eligible: yes/no, beneficiary ID, family ID, remaining balance | 30 min - 2 hours |
| D13 | Consultation note (hub) | EMR entry or handwritten | Medical oncologist | Reviews outside reports, clinical exam, plan: "repeat IHC, FISH, staging CT, echo, then treatment plan" | Same day |

**THE IDENTITY PROBLEM**: Meera now has TWO MRNs (spoke D1, hub D11) and a PM-JAY ID (D12). Her name may be spelled differently ("Meera Devi" vs "Mira Devi"). No ABHA linking. If she ever returns to the spoke hospital, the records won't automatically connect.

**What the oncologist finds reviewing the folder**: D8 and D9 exist but HER2 is equivocal and FISH was never done. The imaging (D3, D4) is from 3-4 weeks ago — acceptable. The biopsy slides need to be reviewed by the hub's own pathologist.

### Week 7-8 — Repeat/additional diagnostics at hub

**Why repeat**: Hub hospital often re-reviews biopsy slides and may redo IHC if quality is questionable or the lab isn't accredited. FISH is done here for the first time.

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D14 | Slide review request | Digital (EMR) | Oncologist | "Review outside slides, repeat IHC if needed, add FISH" | Same day |
| D15 | Slide review + FISH report | Digital/printed, 2-3 pages | Hub pathologist | "Concur with IDC Grade 2. IHC: ER+, PR+, HER2 2+. FISH: **POSITIVE** (HER2/CEP17 ratio 2.8, copy number 6.2). Final: HER2-positive." | **5-7 working days for slide review + IHC, additional 5-7 for FISH = up to 14 days total** |
| D16 | Staging CT (chest/abdomen/pelvis) | Digital (PACS) + printed report | Radiologist | No distant metastasis. Axillary LN 1.5 cm. TNM: cT2N1M0, Stage IIB | 2-3 days for appointment + 1-2 days for report |
| D17 | Baseline echocardiogram | Digital + printed report | Cardiologist | LVEF 62%, normal wall motion. GLS not done (not available at this centre). | 1-3 days for appointment + same-day report |
| D18 | Baseline CBC + metabolic panel | Digital (lab system) | Lab | WBC 7,200/µL, ANC 4,100/µL, Hb 11.2 g/dL, platelets 2.1 lakh/µL, creatinine 0.8 mg/dL, bilirubin 0.6 mg/dL, AST 28 U/L, ALT 32 U/L | Same day or next day |

**FISH takes the longest.** Meera arrived in Delhi on Week 7. FISH result comes back on Week 9. She and her husband have been in Delhi for 2 weeks, staying with a relative, spending money every day, unable to work. This is the "diagnostic pilgrimage" documented in PMC12374521 — 74% of patients visit ≥2 facilities.

**Cost at hub** (before treatment starts): ₹500 registration + ₹3,000-5,000 consultation + ₹4,000-6,000 FISH + ₹5,000-8,000 CT + ₹2,000-3,000 echo + ₹1,500-2,500 labs = **₹16,000-25,000**. Plus 2 weeks of Delhi living costs (~₹1,500/day × 14 = ₹21,000). Total: **₹37,000-46,000** before any treatment.

**Remaining from ₹2 lakh savings**: approximately ₹1.5-1.6 lakh.

### Week 9 — Tumor board / MDT discussion

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D19 | MDT recommendation | EMR note or paper form | Tumor board | "cT2N1M0 HER2+ breast cancer. Plan: 4 cycles AC → 12 weeks paclitaxel + trastuzumab → surgery → trastuzumab to complete 1 year. Start trastuzumab with taxane phase." | Same day |
| D20 | PM-JAY pre-authorization request | Digital (TMS portal) | PM-JAY desk | Package: "Medical management of Ca breast — chemotherapy", estimated ₹50,000/cycle, supporting docs: D15, D16, D19 | Submitted same day |

### Week 9-10 — PM-JAY pre-authorization

**The wait**: PM-JAY SHA (UP state) must approve the case. Meera is from UP, hospital is in Delhi — this is a cross-state portability case.

| Outcome | Probability | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Approved within 48 hrs | ~40% | Treatment can start |
| Delayed 3-7 days | ~35% | Meera waits in Delhi, burning money |
| Denied — documentation issue | ~15% | Must resubmit with corrected docs |
| Denied — empanelment issue | ~10% | Must find another hospital or pay OOP |

**Documents generated**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D21 | PM-JAY pre-auth approval | Digital (TMS portal) + printed | UP SHA | Approved, package code, approved amount ₹50,000, validity 30 days | 2-7 days |

**If denied**: the family must decide — pay out of pocket (they can't), go to a different hospital (start over), or go home and try again through a different pathway. **This is where 14% of cycles get missed and families sell assets.**

## Phase 3: Treatment — AC phase (Weeks 10–18)

### Regimen: AC-TH (the most common Indian HER2+ adjuvant protocol)
- **AC phase**: Doxorubicin 60 mg/m² + Cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m², every 21 days × 4 cycles
- **TH phase**: Paclitaxel 80 mg/m² weekly × 12 + Trastuzumab (loading 8 mg/kg then 6 mg/kg q3w)
- **Total trastuzumab**: to complete 1 year (17 cycles of q3w)

### Cycle 1 of AC (Week 10)

**Day before (pre-chemo labs)**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D22 | Pre-cycle CBC | Digital (lab) | Lab | WBC 6,800, ANC 3,900, Hb 11.0, Plt 1.9 lakh. **All gates pass.** | Same day |

**Day of cycle**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D23 | Chemo order | EMR | Oncologist | Drug, dose (calculated from BSA), route, rate, pre-medications | Same day |
| D24 | Nursing administration record | EMR or paper | Day-care nurse | Time started, time completed, vital signs during infusion, any reactions | Same day |
| D25 | Day-care discharge note | EMR or paper | Nurse/resident | "Tolerated well. Next cycle: [date]. Return for labs 1 day before." | Same day |

**Post-cycle at home (Meera returns to Jhansi between cycles)**:
- Expected side effects: nausea (managed with anti-emetics), fatigue, hair loss starting week 2-3
- Instructions given verbally and on D25 paper
- Emergency contact number for the hub hospital
- Next cycle date: Week 13 (21 days later)

**Cost**: PM-JAY covers ₹50,000. Actual cost of AC cycle: approximately ₹15,000-20,000 for drugs + ₹5,000-10,000 for day-care. PM-JAY package covers this.

### Between cycles 1 and 2 (Weeks 10-13) — THE GAP

**What should happen**: Someone tracks whether Meera got her labs done in Jhansi 1-2 days before the next cycle, and whether the results are sent to the hub.

**What actually happens**: Nobody tracks this. Meera goes to a lab in Jhansi, gets her CBC done, and brings the paper report in her folder when she travels to Delhi for cycle 2. If she forgets, or if the Jhansi lab uses different units, or if she loses the report, it's discovered at the hub on cycle day.

**THE STAGE 9 GAP**: This is where our system lives. Between every cycle, for every patient on the list: are labs done? Are they within thresholds? Is the PM-JAY authorization still valid? Is the next cycle date confirmed?

### Cycles 2-4 of AC (Weeks 13, 16, 19)

Same pattern. Documents D22-D25 repeated each cycle.

**What changes**:
- Cycle 3: ANC drops to 1,200/µL → **BELOW THRESHOLD (1,500)**. Cycle delayed 1 week. New labs drawn. ANC recovers to 1,800. Cycle proceeds day 8 late.
- Cycle 4: echo not required during AC phase (no trastuzumab yet). PM-JAY authorization needs renewal (original 30-day validity expired). D20-D21 process repeats.

**Document count after AC phase**: ~30 documents in the folder. Some are originals, some are photocopies, some exist only as phone photos. No single system has all of them.

## Phase 4: Treatment — Taxane + Trastuzumab phase (Weeks 20–32)

### First trastuzumab dose (Week 20) — requires additional documentation

**Additional pre-treatment requirements**:
| # | Document | Format | Generated by | Contains | Available when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D26 | Repeat echo (pre-trastuzumab) | Printed report | Cardiologist | LVEF 58% (dropped from 62% baseline — within normal, no hold trigger). | 1-2 days before |
| D27 | FISH report confirmation | Already exists as D15 | — | Verified HER2-positive. **This is the document that was "pending" back in Week 6 and finally arrived in Week 9.** | Already in file |

**If D15 (FISH) had been lost or never done**: trastuzumab cannot start. The treatment plan stalls. This is the exact scenario in demo case P-017.

### Weekly paclitaxel + q3w trastuzumab (Weeks 20-32)

- Paclitaxel: weekly × 12 (weeks 20-31)
- Trastuzumab: loading dose week 20, then q3w starting week 23
- Labs: CBC before each weekly paclitaxel. Echo every 3 months (next at week 32-33).

**Document volume**: 12 weekly lab reports + 4-5 trastuzumab administration records + 1 echo = ~17 additional documents in 12 weeks.

**PM-JAY renewal**: every 30 days. The family must ensure the authorization is current before each visit. This is the coverage gate's domain.

## Phase 6: Adjuvant trastuzumab completion (Weeks 36-72)

Trastuzumab continues q3w to complete 1 year (17 total cycles). At this point, paclitaxel is done and Meera receives trastuzumab alone.

**Every 3 cycles (every ~9 weeks)**: repeat echo (D26 pattern).
**Every cycle**: pre-cycle labs (D22 pattern), administration record (D24 pattern).

**Echo surveillance schedule**:
| When | LVEF | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline (Week 8) | 62% | Documented, proceed |
| Pre-trastuzumab (Week 20) | 58% | Within normal, proceed |
| Week 32 | 55% | Dropped 7% from baseline. Below 55% is borderline. Close monitoring. |
| Week 41 | 52% | **Dropped 10% from baseline AND below institutional LLN (55%).** FDA criteria: hold. |
| Week 45 (4 weeks later) | 50% | No recovery. **Oncologist decides: discontinue permanently or continue with cardioprotection.** This is a Class A decision — our system presents the numbers and defers. |

**If LVEF drops**: this generates a cascade of documents — cardiology referral, cardiology consultation note, medication change (add ACE-inhibitor/beta-blocker), repeat echo in 2-4 weeks, and a treatment modification note.

## Phase 7: Follow-up (Year 1-5 post-treatment)

After completing 1 year of trastuzumab:
- Clinical exam every 3-4 months for first 2 years, then every 6 months
- Annual mammogram
- No routine CT/PET unless symptomatic (per NCG guidelines — NCCN aligns)
- Echo: final at treatment completion, then per cardiology follow-up if LVEF was affected
- Labs: as clinically indicated

## What goes wrong — the failure scenarios to model in synthetic data

| # | Scenario | When | What happens | Detection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | FISH never done | Week 6→forever | HER2 status unknown. Trastuzumab cannot start. Treatment plan incomplete. | Documentation gate: "HER2 IHC 2+ without FISH — final status PENDING" |
| F2 | FISH result not delivered to hub | Week 9 | Hub doesn't know FISH was done at referral lab. Repeats it (cost, time). | Documentation gate: document with accession exists at source but not received |
| F3 | PM-JAY pre-auth delayed | Week 9-10 | Family waits in Delhi burning money. | Coverage gate: authorization status PENDING |
| F4 | PM-JAY pre-auth denied (wrong package) | Week 10 | Hospital selected wrong code. Must resubmit. | Coverage gate: authorization status DENIED, reason: package mismatch |
| F5 | ANC below threshold | Week 16 (Cycle 3) | Cycle delayed. | Clinical gate: ANC < 1500 |
| F6 | Echo stale | Week 32 | Last echo >90 days ago. Trastuzumab hold risk. | Surveillance gate: days since LVEF > 90 |
| F7 | PM-JAY not renewed | Week 25 | Authorization expired. Hospital won't infuse. | Coverage gate: authorization expired |
| F8 | Lab units mismatch | Any cycle | Jhansi lab reports platelets in "lakh/µL", hub expects "×10⁹/L" | Parsing trap: unit normalization |
